Email Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026.

This policy applies to useorionplacement.com and to every email that mentions or links to it. It applies to Orion Placement and to anyone who sends email on our behalf, including affiliates and third parties.

No unsolicited bulk email

Orion Placement prohibits advertising useorionplacement.com, or any other website we operate, through unsolicited bulk email or spam. Anyone who sends email for us is bound by this rule.

How we send email

We send individually targeted business email, such as an introduction to an employer about recruiting services or a reply to someone who has contacted us. We use accurate sender names and addresses, and we do not use misleading subject lines.

Stopping email

If you do not want to hear from us, reply to the message or contact us and ask us to stop. We will honor the request promptly and will not contact you again about our services.

Reporting abuse

If you received email that you believe was sent in violation of this policy, send us the full message through our contact page. We will investigate and take action, including ending the sender’s access.
