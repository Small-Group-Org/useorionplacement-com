How we recruit

A recruitment process built for precision and efficiency.

  1. Step 1

    In-depth consultation

    We take the time to understand your company’s needs, culture and specific hiring goals.

  2. Step 2

    Proactive talent search

    Through our network, we identify and approach highly qualified professionals, including those not actively seeking new roles.

  3. Step 3

    Streamlined selection

    We manage the recruitment journey and present the most qualified professionals to your company.

  4. Step 4

    Hiring assistance

    From initial interviews to final offer negotiations, we guide you through each stage of the hiring process.

Who we are

Orion Placement is a technology-driven boutique recruiting firm headquartered in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. It was founded in 2022 by Justin Sofman and Jon Missirlian.

Justin Sofman

Co-founder

Justin spent several years consulting enterprise clients at a large tech recruiting firm before co-founding Orion Placement. He takes a consultative approach with clients and his recruiting team.

Jon Missirlian

Co-founder

Jon focuses on candidate sourcing and interviewing, and on building strong relationships with senior leaders to understand their needs and goals.
